## Three Versions, One Name, Zero Clarity

Market Cipher is one of the most-pirated indicator families on TradingView — and one of the most misunderstood. Search "Market Cipher" and you'll find people arguing about A, B, and D like they're console generations. Most articles don't even explain what changed between versions, let alone which one you should pay for.

Here's the truth after reviewing all three: **they are not upgrades of each other. They are three different tools that share a brand name.** Pick the wrong one and you'll blame the indicator when the real problem is the version.

---

## Market Cipher A — The Confluence Engine

Market Cipher A is the original all-in-one: momentum, volume, and trend fused into one dashboard. Its standout feature is **multi-timeframe projection** — it reads the higher-timeframe trend and paints it onto your current chart. On the 5-minute you see the 1-hour direction without flipping tabs.

The other thing A gets right: **no repaint**. We confirmed it by refreshing charts and checking historical bars. What you see is what you get.

The tradeoff is signal frequency. In choppy markets, A goes quiet — it's a confluence filter, not a signal generator. That's why we rated it 4/5: it won't make you a millionaire, but it will keep you out of bad trades.

## Market Cipher B — The Dashboard Version

Market Cipher B takes the same core idea and adds a volatility layer. Three features define it:

1. **The Cipher Wave** — a smoothed momentum line that acts as a trend filter. Above zero and rising? Longs only. Below zero and falling? Shorts only.
2. **Volume divergence paints** — bars where price and volume disagree get highlighted automatically. This is the best early-reversal tool in the family.
3. **The volatility squeeze dot** — a dot on the chart when Bollinger Bands contract below a threshold. It's a compressed-spring alert.

B is the most feature-dense version, and it shows — every component has its own alert, and there's a real learning curve. We docked a star for that and for slight repainting on some signals. It's a 4/5 for traders willing to spend 2–3 hours learning its language. It is *not* for 1-minute scalpers or beginners who want arrow spam.

## Market Cipher D — The Divergence Specialist

Market Cipher D drops some of B's dashboard bloat and doubles down on one thing: **volume divergence detection**. It flags when volume shrinks while price prints a new high or low — fakeout fuel, caught early.

D adds a **cycle meter** (a red-to-green histogram that shifts with a weighted average of momentum, volume, and price action) and a **fast/slow signal line cross** where volume confirmation is required for entry. And like A, it doesn't repaint.

D is the most focused of the three — great for pros who already have a confluence system and just want divergence on tap, confusing for newbies. Also 4/5.

## Which One Should You Install?

The version choice comes down to how you trade, not which is "newest":

- **Pick A** if you want a clean confluence filter with no repaint and a free path in (search "Market Cipher Lite" first). Best for swing traders who want to avoid bad trades more than catch every move.
- **Pick B** if you want the full dashboard — squeeze detection, divergence paints, per-component alerts — and you're willing to learn it. Best for active day traders on 5-minute and up.
- **Pick D** if you already have entries figured out and specifically need volume divergence confirmation. Best for traders who fade moves or trade reversals.

What top articles miss: the versions aren't interchangeable, and "more features" isn't automatically better. A is the most honest, B is the most powerful, D is the most surgical. All three are solid 4/5 tools — the mistake is buying the wrong one for your style and blaming the indicator.
