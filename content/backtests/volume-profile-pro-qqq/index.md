## How the Volume Profile Pro Works

The volume profile pro is one of the most widely-used signals in trading. It's simple, mechanical, and removes emotional decision-making from your trading by generating clear entry and exit signals based on the VWAP indicator.

On QQQ, the VWAP acts as a trend filter. It won't catch every exact bottom, but it reliably captures the meat of major moves while keeping you out of chop.

## 5-Year Performance on QQQ

Over five years (2021-11-29 → 2026-11-27), the Volume Profile Pro delivered a **+42.5% total return** on QQQ, compounding at **7.3% annually**. 

At **0.32**, the VWAP earned less than one units of return for every unit of risk taken. For context, anything above 1.0 is considered good; above 1.5 is excellent.

The catch? A **26.8% max drawdown**. At some point during this 5-year run, you'd have been down nearly 27% from peak equity. That's stomach-churning territory for most traders. If you can't handle that kind of drawdown, this isn't for you — or you need to size down.

The volume profile pro shows moderate edge on QQQ — **0.32 Sharpe, 1.45 profit factor** over 71 trades. It's not a home run, but it's also not random.
