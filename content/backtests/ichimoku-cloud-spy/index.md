## How the Ichimoku Cloud Works

The ichimoku cloud is one of the most widely-used signals in trading. It's simple, mechanical, and removes emotional decision-making from your trading by generating clear entry and exit signals based on the Ichimoku indicator.

On SPY, the Ichimoku acts as a trend filter. It won't catch every exact bottom, but it reliably captures the meat of major moves while keeping you out of chop.

## 5-Year Performance on SPY

Over five years (2021-08-30 → 2026-08-28), the Ichimoku Cloud delivered a **+54.4% total return** on SPY, compounding at **9.1% annually**. 

At **0.54**, the Ichimoku earned less than one units of return for every unit of risk taken. For context, anything above 1.0 is considered good; above 1.5 is excellent.

The catch? A **19.0% max drawdown**. At some point during this 5-year run, you'd have been down nearly 19% from peak equity. That's significant territory for most traders. If you can't handle that kind of drawdown, this isn't for you — or you need to size down.

## Does the Ichimoku Cloud Work on SPY?

The ichimoku cloud shows moderate edge on SPY — **0.54 Sharpe, 2.96 profit factor** over 19 trades. It's not a home run, but it's also not random.

The 52.6% win rate means you'll be wrong more than half the time. The profit factor above 1.0 means your wins are bigger than your losses, which is what keeps you profitable.

Our take: usable as a confirmation tool, but not as a standalone system on SPY. Combine with trend filters or volume confirmation for better results.
