# Deep_Crab Review

Harmonic pattern indicators have a poor reputation, and much of it is earned. Many repaint after the fact, and many flood the chart with signals that carry no edge. *Deep_Crab* is a single-pattern tool in the 07 category, and the case for it rests on doing one job cleanly rather than doing everything.

## What Deep_Crab Actually Does

The indicator automatically detects **Crab harmonic patterns**—the deep retracement variant of the Gartley family. The structure is the familiar XA, AB, BC, and CD leg sequence, defined by Fibonacci ratios (the 0.886 retracement of XA and extensions on the BC leg are the signature of the Crab). Deep_Crab plots the pattern on the chart, marks the potential reversal zone (PRZ), and draws the legs.

There is no AI or machine-learning angle here. It is geometric pattern recognition, and its value depends entirely on whether you can read harmonic structure.

## Key Features

- **Clean visual output** – The lines are kept minimal compared to busier harmonic tools. The PRZ is shown as a shaded zone, and the pattern label indicates the expected reversal direction.
- **Scanning** – It scans across visible timeframes without requiring a manual pattern search.
- **Alerts** – Alerts can be set for when a new Crab pattern forms.
- **Repainting behavior** – Confirmed patterns (all legs complete) hold their lines. Early-stage patterns will adjust as new bars form, which is standard behavior for harmonic indicators.

## Settings and How to Tune Them

- **Pattern sensitivity** – Controls how strictly the Fibonacci ratios must be matched. Looser settings catch more patterns and produce more false signals; tighter settings catch fewer.
- **Minimum leg length** – Filters out short, noisy formations. Longer minimums reduce clutter, particularly on higher timeframes and on thinly traded instruments.
- **Show invalid patterns** – Toggling this off removes broken or failed pattern drawings from the chart.
- **PRZ extension** – The Crab's defining ratio. Keeping it at the Crab value preserves the pattern's identity.

On timeframes, the tool is oriented toward swing horizons rather than very short intraday charts, where pattern formation is slow relative to the noise. Longer timeframes mean patterns take considerably longer to complete.

## Using It for Entries and Exits

A workable process:

1. **Wait for the PRZ touch** – The pattern must be fully formed and price must enter the shaded PRZ zone. No early entries.
2. **Check confluence** – Look for supporting evidence: momentum divergence, a support or resistance level, or a candlestick reversal pattern.
3. **Entry** – A limit order at the defining Fibonacci level within the PRZ, with a stop placed beyond the PRZ extreme.
4. **Targets** – Common objectives are retracements of the CD leg and the origin of the pattern at point X.

Never enter on the pattern alone. Harmonic patterns are probability tools, not guarantees, and a technically perfect formation can fail immediately if the broader trend is too strong.

## Pros

- Focused pattern detection without the clutter of multi-pattern scanners
- Clean chart display
- Alert functionality for pattern completion
- No notable performance drag with many charts open

## Cons

- **Only detects Crab patterns** – Bat, Gartley, and Butterfly require a separate indicator.
- **No multi-timeframe confirmation** – Higher and lower timeframe context must be checked manually.
- **Default settings struggle on low-volume pairs** – Thinly traded instruments need a longer minimum leg length to filter false formations.

## Who Is This For?

Swing and position traders who already work with harmonic analysis. If Fibonacci ratios and PRZ zones are familiar, Deep_Crab removes the manual pattern-hunting work.

It is not suited to scalpers or very short-term intraday traders, since the patterns form too slowly for that style.

## Alternatives

- **Harmonic Patterns Scanner (by LonesomeTheBlue)** – Detects all major patterns (Crab, Bat, Gartley, Butterfly) in one indicator. More versatile, somewhat more cluttered.
- **ZUP (ZigZag Universal Patterns)** – A long-standing harmonic toolkit with a steeper learning curve and an older interface.

If you trade only Crab patterns, Deep_Crab is the more focused choice. If you want a multi-pattern scanner, look at the Harmonic Patterns Scanner.

## FAQ

**Does Deep_Crab repaint?**
Partially. During formation, lines adjust as new bars close. Once all legs are drawn and the pattern is confirmed, it holds.

**Can it be used on crypto?**
Yes. It applies to major pairs and altcoins, though lower-liquidity pairs need a longer minimum leg length to filter false patterns.

**What timeframe is best?**
Swing horizons rather than very short intraday charts. Very low timeframes are noisy, and very high timeframes mean patterns take a long time to complete.

**Is it free?**
It is a paid indicator on TradingView. Pricing is listed in the 07 category.

## Final Verdict

Deep_Crab does one thing—Crab pattern detection—and does it without clutter. It is not a magic button, but for traders already committed to harmonic analysis, it is a focused, time-saving tool. The absence of multi-pattern support is a genuine limitation, but for dedicated Crab traders the tradeoff is reasonable.
