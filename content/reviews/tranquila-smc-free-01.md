**Description:** A review of Tranquila_Smc_Free_01, a free SMC indicator for liquidity sweeps, FVG, and order blocks — what it plots, how to tune it, and where it falls short.

Free SMC indicators have a reputation problem: many promise a full smart-money toolkit and deliver laggy, repainting clutter instead. Tranquila_Smc_Free_01 sits at the better end of that spectrum, but it is not a complete solution. Here is the breakdown.

## What This Indicator Actually Does

Tranquila_Smc_Free_01 plots the core SMC elements automatically:

- **Liquidity levels** – buy-side and sell-side sweeps
- **Fair Value Gaps (FVGs)** – with optional fill detection
- **Order Blocks (OBs)** – marked at swing points
- **Market structure breaks** – simple lines for BOS/CHoCH

The code is lean for a free script, with no bloat or embedded promotion. Visuals are restrained rather than crowded.

## Key Features That Matter

**1. Liquidity sweep detection.** It flags wicks that take out previous highs or lows and marks them with labels, so sweep events are visible without manual drawing.

**2. Directional FVG coloring.** Bullish gaps and bearish gaps are color-coded, and the indicator can show whether a gap has been filled, which helps filter out setups that have already played out.

**3. Order blocks as price zones.** OBs are drawn as defined zones rather than vague boxes, which makes them usable as reference levels for stops.

**4. Readable, minimalist display.** Elements can be toggled off and opacity adjusted, so the chart stays workable.

## Settings and How to Tune Them

- **Timeframe:** The indicator is best suited to intraday timeframes; very low timeframes tend to produce noisy signals.
- **FVG Sensitivity:** Controls how many gaps qualify. Lower values filter out marginal gaps and keep only cleaner ones.
- **Liquidity Lookback:** Sets how far back the indicator searches for liquidity levels. Longer lookbacks mark older levels that may no longer be relevant.
- **Order Block Type:** A choice between swing-based and volume-based OBs. Swing mode is the more stable of the two.
- **Show Labels:** Can be turned off to reduce on-chart text when working on fast timeframes.

## How to Use It for Entries and Exits

**Entry setup:**
1. Wait for a liquidity sweep to be labeled.
2. Check whether price is at an FVG or order block following the sweep.
3. Enter on the first candle that closes back inside the FVG or OB zone.

**Exit:** Place the stop just beyond the order block or FVG edge. Take partial profits at the next liquidity level.

## Honest Pros and Cons

**Pros:**
- Free, with no paywall or premium upsell.
- Clean visual design that does not overwhelm the chart.
- Usable across forex, crypto, and indices.

**Cons:**
- **No multi-timeframe alignment.** It only reflects the current chart's structure, so higher-timeframe context must come from elsewhere.
- **Occasional false FVGs in ranging markets.** In choppy conditions, small gaps are best ignored.
- **No alert system.** The chart has to be watched manually.
- **No support or updates.** It is a free script, so don't expect maintenance.

## Who Is This For?

**Yes, if:**
- You trade SMC/ICT concepts and want a quick visual aid.
- You need liquidity and FVG detection without paying for it.
- You work on higher intraday timeframes and don't need alerts.

**No, if:**
- You scalp very low timeframes, where the signal-to-noise ratio degrades.
- You require built-in multi-timeframe confirmation.
- You want a fully automated system — this is a tool, not a robot.

## Alternatives

- **LuxAlgo SMC Suite** (paid) – more features, multi-timeframe support, and alerts.
- **ICT Killer** (free) – similar functionality but heavier on the chart.
- **Manual SMC** – if you know the concepts, drawing your own levels gives you more control. The indicator is a time-saver, not a replacement for understanding the model.

## FAQ

**Does Tranquila_Smc_Free_01 repaint?**
The indicator itself is designed not to repaint on confirmed bars, but FVG boxes can shift slightly before the candle closes. Wait for the close before acting.

**Can I use it on crypto?**
Yes, it works on major coins. On low-cap coins with thin liquidity, the gaps carry little meaning.

**Why don't I see any liquidity labels?**
Check the lookback period — if it is set too short, sweeps will be missed. Quiet sessions on some pairs also simply produce no clean sweeps.

**Is there a paid version?**
No. There is only the free version.

## Final Verdict

Tranquila_Smc_Free_01 does what it advertises and not much more. It handles the mechanical work of marking liquidity, FVGs, and order blocks, and it does so without cluttering the chart. It will not make anyone profitable on its own — no indicator will — but as a time-saver for SMC setups, it holds up.

The absence of multi-timeframe support and alerts is what keeps it short of the top tier. As a free tool, though, the tradeoffs are reasonable.

**Worth installing?** Yes, as a secondary confirmation layer alongside your own analysis. It is a helper, not a holy grail.
