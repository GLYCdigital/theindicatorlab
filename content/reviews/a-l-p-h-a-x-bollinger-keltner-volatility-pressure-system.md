## What it actually does

Bollinger Bands and Keltner Channels are both volatility envelopes wrapped around a moving average. They look similar but measure volatility differently — Bollinger Bands are driven by standard deviation, Keltner Channels by average true range. Stacking them lets you compare two volatility reads at once.

The "pressure system" framing is the interesting part. The classic technique here is the squeeze: when the Bollinger Bands contract inside the Keltner Channels, volatility is compressed and a breakout often follows. That concept — volatility coiling before expansion — is the conceptual backbone of any tool with this name.

So the honest description is this: a trend-category indicator that overlays two volatility band systems and uses their interaction to flag compression and expansion. Whether it adds proprietary logic on top, I can't confirm from the material available.

## Key features

- **Dual volatility envelopes.** Bollinger and Keltner plotted together, which is the whole point — you're comparing two measures of range, not one.
- **Pressure/compression focus.** The design centers on the idea that low volatility precedes directional moves, which is a legitimate trend-entry concept.
- **Trend category.** It sits in TradingView's trend bucket, so expect it to lean toward signaling direction rather than pure mean-reversion.
- **Visual band logic.** Because both envelopes are on-chart, you can read the relationship directly without a separate oscillator panel.

## How to use it

The workflow this concept supports is straightforward. Watch for the two bands to converge or for one to contain the other — that's the compression read. Then watch for price to break out of the coil as volatility expands. Trend traders use that expansion as a directional cue and the bands themselves as dynamic support/resistance afterward.

As shown in the chart above, the value is in the visual relationship between the envelopes rather than any single line. Treat it as a context tool: it tells you when the market is coiling and when it's releasing, and you pair that with your own entry trigger. Don't expect it to hand you a complete trade plan on its own.
