**Final Verdict: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4/5) — A solid, no-nonsense tool for monthly level trading.**

Let's cut the fluff. Plenty of "auto-support-resistance" indicators out there carry baggage — repainting, lag, opaque calculations. The Previous_Month_High_Low indicator (PMHL for short) does exactly what its name says: it plots the previous month's high and low as horizontal lines on your chart. The levels are static and update at the start of each new month.

### Key Features

- **Levels are fixed once the month closes.** The indicator does not recalculate past values, so historical levels stay put.
- **Automatic monthly reset.** At the start of a new month, the lines shift to the previous month's data. No manual redrawing.
- **Customizable styling.** Line color, thickness, and style are adjustable.
- **Works across timeframes.** The levels can be displayed on anything from intraday charts up through weekly.
- **Lightweight.** The calculation is trivial, so it places minimal load on a chart.

### Settings and How to Tune Them

The indicator exposes a small set of display options rather than trading parameters:

- **Line Style** — solid, dashed, or similar, depending on your platform's options. Dashed tends to read more cleanly on busy charts.
- **High Color / Low Color** — set independently so the two levels are visually distinct.
- **Line Width** — thicker lines are easier to spot when the chart is crowded.
- **Extend Lines** — when enabled, the levels stretch across the full chart rather than only near the current bar.

None of these change the underlying levels; they only change how the levels are drawn. Treat them as readability choices, not as performance levers.

### How to Use It for Entries and Exits

This is a level indicator, not a signal generator. It provides reference points; the trade decision is yours. Common approaches:

- **Breakout:** A close beyond the previous month's high can be treated as a breakout candidate. The previous month's low is the natural invalidation level.
- **Reversal:** A touch of the previous month's low followed by a reversal candle (hammer, bullish engulfing) can be read as a bounce setup, with a stop below the level.
- **Scaling:** Monthly levels work as partial profit targets — for example, trimming part of a position at the previous month's high and letting the remainder run.

### Honest Pros and Cons

**Pros:**
- Dead simple. No learning curve.
- Asset-agnostic — usable on crypto, forex, stocks, and futures.
- Useful for swing traders who want clean reference points.
- Free.

**Cons:**
- Only two lines per month. No intra-month pivots.
- Too wide to be useful on very low timeframes.
- No built-in alerts. Price alerts have to be set manually at the level.
- On volatile assets, monthly levels can be broken multiple times, producing false breakouts.

### Who Is This Actually For?

- **Swing traders** who want clear monthly support/resistance.
- **Position traders** working monthly ranges.
- **Beginners** who want a simple, non-repainting level system.

**Not for:** Scalpers or day traders who need dynamic levels.

### Better Alternatives (If PMHL Doesn't Cut It)

- **Monthly Open High Low (MOHL)** — Similar, but also plots the monthly open. More complete picture.
- **Daily High Low** — Better suited to intraday trading.
- **Auto Fibonacci Retracement** — If you want more levels than just high and low.

### FAQ

**Q: Does this repaint?**
A: Once the month closes, the levels are fixed.

**Q: Can I use it on crypto?**
A: Yes. It works on any pair with monthly data.

**Q: How do I set alerts?**
A: Not from the indicator. Set a manual price alert at the monthly high or low.

**Q: Does it work on lower timeframes?**
A: It can be displayed there, but the levels are wide, so higher timeframes are the more natural fit.

### Final Thoughts

The Previous_Month_High_Low indicator is a tool, not a strategy. It gives you two clean reference levels per month. If you're a swing trader who wants no-nonsense support/resistance, it does that job. If you want a fully automated trading system, look elsewhere.

**Rating: 4/5** — Loses a star for no alerts and no intra-month pivots.
