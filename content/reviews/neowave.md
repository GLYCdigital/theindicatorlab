Neowave is a volume-weighted momentum tool that aims to respect price action without operating as a black box. It sits in a middle ground among indicators—neither obviously repainting nonsense nor hopelessly overengineered.

## What Neowave Actually Does

Neowave isn’t a simple RSI clone. It’s built on volume and price acceleration. The core logic measures the rate of change in volume-weighted price movement, then plots a histogram and a signal line. The premise is that momentum is only meaningful when backed by volume.

The indicator gives you a histogram (green/red bars) and a moving average line. Crossovers and divergences are the main trade signals. It also has an optional alert system for when the histogram flips color.

## Key Features That Set It Apart

- **Volume-weighted momentum** – Most momentum indicators ignore volume; this one doesn’t, which can help filter out low-volume noise.
- **Divergence detection** – It highlights hidden and regular divergences automatically on the chart. Not perfectly, but more thoroughly than many alternatives.
- **Customizable smoothing** – You can adjust the moving average length and volume weight factor. Defaults may work, but tuning is possible for different markets.
- **Multi-timeframe coherence** – Commonly used on higher intraday timeframes where volume data is more reliable.

## Settings and How to Tune Them

- **Momentum length**: A standard lookback is used by default, with longer lengths available for slower trends.
- **Signal line length**: A short signal line is the default.
- **Volume weight factor**: Controls how much volume influences the momentum reading. A higher factor makes the indicator more sensitive to volume.
- **Divergence sensitivity**: Medium sensitivity is the default; lowering it may produce more signals.

There is no single best configuration. Parameter choices depend on the asset and timeframe, and different markets may respond better to different volume weight factors.

## How to Use It for Entries and Exits

**Long entry:**
- Histogram turns green (momentum positive)
- Histogram crosses above signal line
- Price makes a higher low while histogram makes a lower low—a hidden bullish divergence. Enter on confirmation.

**Exit:**
- Histogram turns red and crosses below signal line
- Or a regular bearish divergence on a higher timeframe

**Short entry:**
- Histogram turns red
- Cross below signal line
- Regular bearish divergence (price higher, histogram lower)

**Stop loss:** Place below the most recent swing low for longs, or above the swing high for shorts. The indicator itself doesn’t provide levels.

## Honest Pros and Cons

**Pros:**
- Volume integration can make it more informative than pure momentum oscillators
- Divergence markers save screen time
- Designed to work across asset classes (crypto, futures, forex)
- No repainting reported by the developer

**Cons:**
- Doesn’t give exact entry prices—you need price action confirmation
- Divergence detection isn’t perfect; false signals can appear in choppy markets
- Learning curve: the volume weight concept isn’t intuitive for beginners
- Not well suited to scalping on very low timeframes

## Who It’s Actually For

- Swing traders on higher intraday timeframes who want volume confirmation
- Traders who already use RSI or MACD and want a volume-based alternative
- Anyone trading high-volume assets (crypto, indices, forex majors)

**Not for:**
- Scalpers (too slow)
- Beginners who want a “buy/sell” button
- Low-volume pairs (like some altcoins or exotic forex)

## Better Alternatives

- **Volume Weighted MACD** – Similar concept, but simpler and more established.
- **VWAP + RSI** – Less sophisticated but more reliable for intraday.
- **OBV + Momentum** – Free alternative that also uses volume.

Neowave isn’t a must-have, but if you already use volume-based analysis, it’s a reasonable addition.

## FAQ

**Q: Does Neowave repaint?**
A: The developer states it does not. Values are fixed once the bar closes.

**Q: Can I use it for crypto?**
A: Yes. A higher volume weight factor may be appropriate. Works best on high-volume crypto assets.

**Q: What timeframe is best?**
A: Higher intraday timeframes. Lower timeframes tend to give more false signals.

**Q: Does it give buy/sell alerts?**
A: Yes, but only for histogram color change. Divergence alerts must be set manually.

## Final Verdict

Neowave is a solid volume-based momentum indicator that avoids the worst sins (repainting, lag, complexity). It’s not revolutionary, but it can be reliable if you know how to read divergence and use volume context.

It’s a good tool, not a holy grail. For many traders, it’s worth adding to a toolkit—but not relying on alone.
