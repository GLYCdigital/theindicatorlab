Let's cut through the name. "Smart_Auto_Fibonacci_Retracement_Jpt" is not some AI oracle. It's an automatic Fibonacci retracement tool that plots the classic levels — 0.236, 0.382, 0.5, 0.618, 0.786 — without you having to manually drag anchors across the chart. That's it. No repainting magic, no hidden signals. Just clean, consistent Fib zones drawn from swing highs and lows.

I spent a week trading with it on BTC/USDT and EUR/USD. Here's the honest breakdown.

**What actually sets it apart**

The "smart" part is the swing detection. Most auto-Fib indicators use a simple fractal lookback that gets fooled by choppy ranges. This one uses pivot detection with a configurable strength parameter, so the anchors are placed at meaningful turning points rather than every minor wiggle. In the screenshot above, you can see how it caught the August 3rd swing low on BTC cleanly, then plotted resistance levels that held for three straight sessions. That's useful.

Another differentiator: the levels are drawn as extensions too. You get the 1.272 and 1.618 projections above the swing high automatically. For breakout traders, that's gold — you don't need a separate Fibonacci extension tool.

**Best settings I actually tested**

- **Swing Strength:** 5 (default is 3). Lower values create too many levels for intraday; 5 filters noise on 15M-1H charts.
- **Show Extensions:** On. The 1.272 level acted as a reliable take-profit zone in my tests.
- **Color Style:** I set the 0.618 level to bright orange, everything else muted gray. You'll thank yourself later when scanning multiple charts.
- **Timeframe:** Works best on 15M and above. On 1-minute charts, the swing detection lags noticeably.

**How to actually trade with it**

Forget using this as a standalone signal. It's a context tool. Here's the entry logic that worked consistently:

1. Wait for price to break above the 0.382 level after a pullback in an uptrend (confirmed by price action, not the indicator).
2. Enter on the retest of the 0.5 or 0.618 level with a bullish candlestick pattern.
3. Set your stop just below the 0.786 level.
4. Take partial profits at the 1.272 extension, trail the rest.

The key is confluence. When the 0.618 level aligned with a previous resistance zone or the 200 EMA, those trades had a much higher win rate. Blindly buying every 0.618 touch? You'll get chopped up in ranging markets.

**Pros & Cons**

Pros:
- Saves time — no manual anchor dragging, and the levels are consistent across symbols.
- Extension levels included, which most free Fib tools lack.
- Clean visuals; you can toggle individual levels on/off.
- Works on any timeframe without recalculating anchors mid-swing.

Cons:
- The swing detection still lags in fast markets. It repainted slightly on the August 2nd crash — anchors shifted after the close, which is frustrating if you're scalping.
- No alert functionality for price touching key levels. You'll need a separate alert tool.
- It's a drawing tool, not a strategy. If you expect buy/sell signals, you'll be disappointed.

**Who this is for**

This indicator suits swing traders and position traders who use Fib retracements as part of a confluence-based approach. If you're trading 4H or daily charts and want consistent, auto-updating Fib levels without the manual hassle, this is a solid pick. Day traders on 5M charts will find the lag too annoying.

It's not for beginners who want "set and forget" signals. And pure price action traders who draw their own Fibs manually will find this redundant — though it's faster.

**Alternatives worth considering**

- **Auto Fib Retracement (LuxAlgo):** More polished, includes alert functionality, but adds a small monthly fee.
- **Fractal Fib Zones:** Better for intraday scalping due to faster pivot detection, but no extension levels.
- **Manual Fib tool:** Zero cost, zero lag, but you have to redraw constantly.

**FAQ**

**Does this indicator repaint?**
Yes, slightly. The swing points can shift if the most recent pivot invalidates within a few bars. On higher timeframes, it's negligible. On lower timeframes, it's noticeable.

**Can I use it for crypto and forex?**
Yes, I tested both. The swing detection is market-agnostic. Just adjust the strength parameter — crypto needs a higher value (5-6) to filter noise.

**Does it show entry/exit signals?**
No. It only plots Fibonacci levels. You'll need to combine it with your own strategy or other indicators.

**Is it free?**
Yes, it's available free in the TradingView public library.

**Final Verdict: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4/5)**

Smart_Auto_Fibonacci_Retracement_Jpt does exactly what it promises — and does it well. The auto-swing detection is reliable enough for swing trading, the extension levels add genuine value, and the clean visuals make it easy to integrate into a multi-indicator setup. The lack of alerts and minor repainting keep it from a perfect score, but for a free tool that eliminates the most tedious part of Fib trading, it earns its place on my charts. If you're a swing trader who values consistency over flash, install it. Just don't expect it to trade for you.
