### What This Indicator Actually Does

Elliott_Structure_Rc_Gabremoku is a script that automatically labels Elliott Wave counts on your chart in real time. It scans price action, identifies swing points, and assigns wave numbers (1-5, A-B-C) based on standard EW rules.

The core output is a series of labels (blue for impulse, red for corrective) and trend lines connecting the waves. No magic—just math applied to your bar data.

---

### Key Features That Set It Apart

- **Real-time wave labeling**: Unlike manual drawing tools, this updates as price moves.
- **Customizable degree**: You can set the minimum swing size (pips/points) to filter noise.
- **Multi-timeframe awareness**: It doesn't just label the current chart—it references higher-degree structure, and can flag retracements that hold key Fibonacci levels.

---

### Settings and How to Tune Them

- **Swing Sensitivity**: Raising this value reduces false labels on choppy markets, while lower values produce more micro-labels. Tune it upward when a chart is noisy.
- **Label Style**: A "Compact" mode keeps the chart readable; the default "Full" mode displays every sub-wave and can clutter the chart.
- **Show Internal Structure**: Best left enabled on higher timeframes only. On lower timeframes it adds noise.

These are conceptual adjustments—there is no single configuration that suits every market or timeframe, and none should be treated as producing better results than another.

---

### How to Use It for Entries & Exits

This isn't a "buy when it says buy" indicator. Treat it as a confluence tool.

**Entry Example (Long):**
Wait for a clear Wave 3 label (blue, no overlap with Wave 1). Then look for a Wave 4 retracement that holds above the Wave 1 high (0.382–0.618 fib). Enter on the first break of the Wave 4 trend line.

**Exit Example:**
When Wave 5 labels appear and price starts diverging from momentum (RSI/volume), take partial profits. The indicator will often re-label Wave 5 as an A-B-C if the count fails—that's your cue to exit the rest.

**Stop Loss Logic:**
Below the Wave 2 low (longs) or above Wave 2 high (shorts). The indicator draws these levels automatically—no mental math.

### Honest Pros and Cons

**Pros:**
- Saves hours of manual labeling.
- Consistent logic—no emotional bias.
- Applies across forex, futures, and crypto.

**Cons:**
- Lag on fast moves.
- False counts in ranging markets—it can label a triangle as an impulse wave.
- No audible alerts—you have to keep your eyes on the screen.

---

### Who This Is Actually For

- **Intermediate-to-advanced Elliott Wave traders** who already understand structure but want automation.
- **Swing traders** on higher timeframes. Scalpers will find it frustrating.
- **Traders who backtest EW strategies** and need consistent labeling for data export.

**Not for:** Beginners who don't know what a Wave 3 is. This tool won't teach you Elliott Wave—it just labels it.

---

### Better Alternatives If This Doesn't Fit

- **Elliott Wave Prophet** (paid, heavier on CPU).
- **Manual drawing + fib tool** (free, zero lag, but requires skill).
- **Auto Fib Retracements** (simpler, less prone to over-labeling).

### FAQ

**Q: Does it work on crypto?**
A: It can, but crypto's volatility causes more re-labeling. Consider raising the swing sensitivity.

**Q: Can I use it for forex scalping?**
A: Not recommended. The lag on low timeframes makes it unreliable for quick entries.

**Q: How do I know if the count is correct?**
A: Cross-check with volume and momentum. If Wave 3 is labeled but volume is declining, the count is likely wrong.

**Q: Does it repaint?**
A: It can update on new bars, and labels tend to solidify after a few bars. Don't enter on the first label—wait for confirmation.

Elliott_Structure_Rc_Gabremoku is a solid tool for any trader who uses Elliott Wave and wants to reduce subjectivity. It's not perfect—the lag on lower timeframes and false counts in ranges are real issues—but for higher-timeframe swing trading, it's a clean automated labeler.
