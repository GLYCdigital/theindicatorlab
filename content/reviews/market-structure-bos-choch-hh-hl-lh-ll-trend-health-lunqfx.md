Let me be blunt: this is a smart indicator with a terrible name. "Market_Structure_Bos_Choch_Hh_Hl_Lh_Ll_Trend_Health_Lunqfx" reads like a keyboard smash, but underneath that clunky title is one of the more thorough market structure tools I've tested this year. I ran it across multiple timeframes on the MACD chart type (as the screenshot demonstrates) and here's what actually matters.

**What It Does (The Real Version)**

This indicator automates the structural analysis most price action traders do manually. It identifies Break of Structure (BOS), Change of Character (CHOCH), and labels the swing points: Higher Highs (HH), Higher Lows (HL), Lower Highs (LH), and Lower Lows (LL). The "Trend Health" part is the differentiator — it grades the current trend's strength based on the sequence and proximity of those swings, not just direction.

The chart above shows how it handles a choppy MACD setup. Notice the swing labels don't get repainted haphazardly. The indicator waits for confirmation before marking a shift, which is the single biggest reason I didn't rate it lower.

**Key Features That Stand Out**

- **Trend Health Gauge**: Rather than just telling you "bullish" or "bearish," it assigns a health score. A sequence of HH/HL with strong momentum gets marked differently than one that's barely making new highs. This filters out weak trends that usually trap breakout traders.
- **BOS vs CHOCH Distinction**: Many indicators blur these. This one correctly labels BOS as a continuation signal and CHOCH as a potential reversal — critical for knowing whether to add to a position or exit.
- **Clean Visual Hierarchy**: Swing points are color-coded and sized by significance. The most recent structure is emphasized, older swings fade. You're never guessing which label matters.
- **Noise Filtering**: The internal swing detection threshold prevents every tiny wick from becoming a structure point. On the MACD chart, it ignored the micro-oscillations and caught the meaningful pivots.

**Best Settings (Tested)**

Default settings work, but I found these tweaks improve reliability:

- **Swing Strength**: Increase from default to 3-4 on lower timeframes (5m-15m). Reduces false labels in volatile sessions.
- **Trend Health Sensitivity**: Set to "Balanced" rather than "Aggressive" — the aggressive mode flips signals too quickly in ranging markets.
- **Show Labels**: Keep "All Labels" enabled initially to understand the logic. Once familiar, switch to "Recent Only" to declutter.

**How I Actually Trade With It**

The entry logic that made sense to me: wait for a CHOCH in the direction of the higher timeframe trend, then confirm with a BOS. The Trend Health gauge must read "Strong" or "Healthy" — if it says "Weak," skip the trade regardless of the structure signal.

For exits, I use the opposite: a CHOCH against my position is the first warning, a BOS confirms exit. The indicator's labels make this mechanical rather than subjective.

**Pros & Cons**

*Pros:*
- Eliminates manual structure marking — huge time saver
- Trend Health filter genuinely improves win rate by avoiding weak setups
- No repainting on confirmed swings (tested extensively)
- Works across all timeframes, though it shines on 15m-4H

*Cons:*
- The name is unusable — you'll need to rename it in your favorites
- Overwhelming on the default settings; every swing point gets a label
- In ranging markets, the Trend Health gauge oscillates between "Weak" and "Neutral" — not helpful for chop
- No alert system for structure changes (a missed opportunity)

**Who It's For**

This is built for traders who already understand market structure but want automation. If you're a beginner still learning what a CHOCH is, this indicator will confuse you more than help — the labels assume you know what they mean. If you're an intermediate or advanced trader who's tired of drawing lines manually, this is a solid upgrade.

**Alternatives Worth Considering**

- **Smart Money Concepts by LuxAlgo**: Better for order blocks and liquidity zones, but less focused on trend health.
- **SMC Structure by lonesomeTheBlue**: Lighter and faster, but lacks the health gauge.
- **Market Structure by zeiierman**: Cleaner visuals, but no BOS/CHOCH distinction.

**FAQ**

**Does it repaint?** Confirmed swings don't repaint. Unconfirmed swings may shift, but that's standard for structure indicators.

**Best timeframe?** 15-minute to 4-hour. On 1-minute it generates too many labels; on weekly it's too slow to be actionable.

**Can it be used for crypto?** Yes, especially on BTC/ETH pairs where structure is cleaner than forex.

**Does it work with the MACD chart type?** Yes, as shown in the screenshot. The structure logic is chart-type agnostic, though it works best on standard candles.

**Final Verdict**

This is a genuinely useful tool that solves a real problem — manually marking structure takes time and introduces subjectivity. The Trend Health feature adds a filter most competitors lack. It's not perfect: the name is terrible, the default settings need adjustment, and it's useless in chop. But for a trader who understands structure and wants to automate the busywork, this earns its place in your toolkit.

**Rating: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4/5)** — Solid, functional, and one tweak away from exceptional. If the developer adds alerts and fixes the naming mess, this becomes a 5-star essential.
