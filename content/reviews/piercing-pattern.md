A solid, no-nonsense candlestick pattern detector for bullish reversals. It won't make you a millionaire, but it saves you from scanning dozens of charts manually.

---

**What This Indicator Actually Does**

Piercing_Pattern automatically identifies the classic two-candle bullish reversal pattern on your chart. You know the setup: a long red candle (bearish) followed by a green candle that opens lower but closes above the midpoint of the previous red body. The indicator places a label (usually an arrow or "PP") right at the pattern completion, so you can spot it without squinting at every candle.

This is **not** a predictive tool—it's a pattern scanner. It shows you what already happened. The real work is on you: deciding if the pattern matters in context.

---

**Key Features That Set It Apart**

- **Customizable body-length filters** – You can require the first candle to meet a minimum size relative to the average candle. This helps filter fake patterns on quiet, choppy days.
- **Highlight candle colors** – The indicator can recolor the two candles so they're impossible to miss. For many traders this is more useful than the default arrow alone.
- **Alert system** – Set it to notify you when a new pattern appears, so you can catch setups without staring at the screen.
- **Minimal chart clutter** – Unlike some pattern indicators that flood the chart with text, this one stays clean. Just a small label or arrow.

---

**Settings and How to Tune Them**

- **Minimum body % of first candle**: This sets how large the first candle's body must be relative to average candle size. A higher threshold filters out small-bodied red candles that don't represent real selling pressure. A threshold of zero lets nearly everything through.
- **Shadow rejection check**: When enabled, this requires the second candle's wick to show buying rejection at the open—useful for avoiding fakeouts.
- **Label style**: Choose between an arrow or a text label. On tight timeframes, text labels can overlap.
- **Alert on completion**: Enable this if you want a notification when a pattern prints.

*Tip*: A very low minimum body threshold floods your chart with marginal patterns during consolidation.

---

**How to Use It for Entries and Exits**

**Entry**
Wait for the arrow to appear. Then confirm:
1. The second candle closed above the midpoint of the first (the indicator checks this automatically).
2. Look for additional confirmation, such as a bullish divergence on RSI or a volume spike on the second candle.
3. Enter on the next candle's open, sized according to your own risk plan.

**Exit**
The pattern itself gives no exit signal. Common approaches are a trailing stop or a fixed target at the previous swing high. If price breaks below the first candle's low, the pattern is invalid.

**Honest Pros and Cons**

**Pros**
- Saves time versus manual scanning.
- Clean, customizable visuals.
- More reliable on liquid markets (indices, majors, crypto).
- Free (no extra cost beyond a TradingView subscription).

**Cons**
- **Laggy by design** – The pattern needs two completed candles, so you're entering after the move has started.
- **Weak in sideways markets** – Patterns during consolidation are frequently false.
- **No volume integration** – A piercing pattern on low volume is often a trap, and the indicator doesn't warn you.
- **Not for scalping** – Too slow for very short timeframes; better suited to higher ones.

---

**Who It's Actually For**

- **Swing traders** who hold positions for days. The pattern is best suited to higher timeframes.
- **Discretionary reversal traders** who already know support/resistance and just want pattern confirmation.
- **New traders** learning candlestick patterns (a useful educational tool).

**Not for**: Scalpers, algorithmic traders (too subjective), or anyone trading illiquid instruments where liquidity undermines pattern reliability.

**FAQ (Real Trader Questions)**

**Q: Does this work on crypto?**
A: It works best on the more liquid pairs. Less liquid altcoins carry more noise and more false signals.

**Q: Can I use it for shorting?**
A: No. This only detects the bullish piercing pattern. For bearish reversals, look for "Dark Cloud Cover" indicators.

**Q: The arrow keeps repainting. Is that normal?**
A: The arrow appears only after the second candle closes. Once it's set, it won't move. That's not repainting—it's confirmation delay.

**Q: Does it work on Heikin Ashi?**
A: It's best avoided. Heikin Ashi smooths data and can produce false patterns. Stick to standard candles.
