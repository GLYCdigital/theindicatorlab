Streak Reversal is a trend-category indicator built around a simple, old idea: markets rarely move in one direction forever. Consecutive same-direction candles — a "streak" — build up, and at some point the pressure that produced them tends to fade. The indicator packages that observation into a visual tool that highlights when a run of like-coloured bars has extended far enough to warrant attention.

That's the concept in its entirety. There are no exotic calculations to explain here, and that's part of the appeal.

## The core idea

Most trend tools try to tell you where price is going. Streak Reversal does something narrower: it points at where a move may be getting stretched. A long string of green candles is not a buy signal in itself — it's a warning that the easy part of the move may already be behind you. The indicator visualises that condition so you don't have to count bars by eye.

This is a mean-reversion instinct applied to trend structure. It is not a forecasting engine and it does not claim to call tops and bottoms with precision. It flags conditions. What you do with that flag is your call.

## How it fits a workflow

The natural use is as a filter or a timing aid layered on top of a trend read you already trust. If you trade pullbacks, a streak marker can tell you when a counter-trend entry is worth considering. If you ride trends, it can suggest when to tighten a stop or take partial profit rather than adding.

It is not a standalone system. Nothing here generates entry and exit pairs on its own, and treating a streak count as a mechanical signal is how traders get run over — strong trends can extend far longer than a streak counter finds comfortable. Used as context, it earns its place on the chart. Used as a trigger, it will frustrate you.

As shown in the chart above, the tool reads cleanly on a standard candlestick layout and doesn't clutter the price panel with boxes and labels competing for attention.

## What works

- **Honest scope.** It does one thing and doesn't pretend otherwise. No black-box scoring, no "AI" branding.
- **Low cognitive load.** A streak is intuitive. You can explain the logic to another trader in one sentence, which matters when you're making decisions under pressure.
- **Complements trend-following tools.** It answers a different question than a moving average or a channel does, so it adds information rather than duplicating it.
- **Useful on multiple timeframes.** Because the concept is timeframe-agnostic, the same logic applies whether you're watching intraday bars or daily ones.

## Where it falls short

- **Streaks lie in strong trends.** The single biggest weakness. In a powerful directional move, consecutive candles can pile up well past the point where a reversal-minded trader would have exited. The indicator will keep flagging, and the trend will keep going.
- **No built-in confirmation.** You are responsible for pairing the streak signal with something else — support and resistance, momentum divergence, volume — to judge whether exhaustion is real.
- **Thin public documentation.** The official listing doesn't spell out much, so expect to spend time watching how it behaves on your own instruments before leaning on it.
- **Mean-reversion bias.** It is structurally inclined to fight strong moves, which is precisely the wrong instinct in a breakout market.

## Who it's for

Discretionary traders who already read trend structure and want a lightweight prompt for when a move is extended. Swing traders working pullbacks will likely get the most value. Pure trend-followers who never fade strength will find it redundant. Beginners should be cautious — the tool rewards judgement, not obedience.

## FAQ

**Does it tell me when to buy or sell?**
No. It highlights extended streaks. The decision to act, and the confirmation you require, is yours.

**Can I use it alone?**
You can, but that's not how it's designed to add value. Pair it with your existing trend or momentum read.

**Does it repaint?**
The concept is based on completed candles, so the signal is descriptive of what has already happened rather than predictive of what comes next. Treat it accordingly.

**Which markets does it suit?**
The logic is instrument-agnostic. How well it works depends on how streaky your chosen market tends to be.

## Verdict

Streak Reversal is a focused, unpretentious tool that does exactly what its name suggests. It won't revolutionise your trading and it won't hand you signals on a plate, but it fills a genuine gap: quantifying trend exhaustion without pretending to predict it. The lack of confirmation mechanics and the inherent weakness in strong trends keep it from a top mark.

⭐⭐⭐⭐ (4/5) — a solid contextual tool for traders who already know how to read a chart.
