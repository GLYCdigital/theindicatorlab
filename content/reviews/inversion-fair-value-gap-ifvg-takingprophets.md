Fair value gaps are one of those concepts that traders either love or dismiss outright. The **Inversion Fair Value Gap Ifvg Takingprophets** indicator sits in the first camp. It takes the standard imbalance idea and focuses specifically on the *inversion* case — the moment a gap that once acted as support flips into resistance, or vice versa. That shift is the whole point of the tool, and it's a cleaner framing than indicators that just plaster every imbalance onto your chart.

## What This Indicator Actually Does

At its core, an inversion fair value gap is a three-candle imbalance that price has already traded back through and violated. When that happens, the zone's role flips. A bullish gap that gets broken becomes a bearish zone, and a bearish gap that gets reclaimed becomes a bullish one. The indicator detects these events and marks the inverted zones on your chart.

That's the mechanic. It isn't trying to predict anything exotic — it's cataloguing a specific structural event and giving you a visual reference for where price might react next. If you already trade order blocks, liquidity sweeps, or supply and demand zones, the logic will feel familiar. The IFVG is essentially a refined version of the same idea, filtered down to gaps that have been reclaimed rather than gaps that are still virgin.

## Key Features

Because no official documentation was available for this listing, I'm describing the concept rather than quoting specific inputs. What the tool offers, based on how IFVG indicators generally function and what the name implies:

- **Automatic detection of inverted fair value gaps** — you don't have to manually mark zones.
- **Zone plotting on the chart** — the inverted gaps are drawn as shaded regions so you can see where price previously flipped a level.
- **Trend-oriented framing** — the indicator is categorised under Trend, which suggests the zones are meant to be read as directional context rather than pure reversal signals.

What sets it apart from a plain FVG tool is the inversion filter. Most fair value gap indicators show you every imbalance. This one narrows the focus to the subset that has been violated — which is a much smaller, and arguably more meaningful, set of levels.

## How to Use It

The workflow is straightforward. You load the indicator, and it marks inverted gaps as they form. From there, the zones act as reference levels:

- In an uptrend, a reclaimed bearish gap becomes a potential demand area to watch for continuation.
- In a downtrend, a broken bullish gap becomes a potential supply area to watch for continuation.
- When price approaches an inverted zone, you're looking for a reaction — rejection, absorption, or a clean pass-through.

The indicator gives you the *where*. The *when* and *whether to act* is still on you. Treat the zones as context, not as buy or sell signals in isolation.

As shown in the chart above, the zones are plotted directly on price, so you can layer them over your existing structure analysis without much visual clutter.
