**What This Indicator Actually Does**

Cypher_Pattern is a harmonic pattern scanner that automatically identifies and plots Cypher patterns on your chart. If you trade XABCD structures, you already know the Cypher is one of the less common harmonic patterns. This indicator removes the manual Fibonacci retracement work from the process.

The indicator draws the complete structure: X-A, A-B, B-C, and C-D legs, plus the potential reversal zone (PRZ). It also labels each leg with the Fibonacci ratios used to confirm the pattern. That is the core value here—the structure is drawn for you rather than measured by hand.

**Key Features That Set It Apart**

- **Auto-detection with ratio validation**: Not every zig-zag is a Cypher. The indicator checks that the X-A leg retraces to 0.618 of the prior move, A-B is 0.382–0.618 of X-A, B-C is 1.13–1.414 of A-B, and C-D extends 0.786 of X-C. If those ratios don't align, the pattern is not drawn.
- **Clear PRZ labels**: It marks the potential reversal zone with a shaded box, so the zone where price is expected to reverse is visible at a glance.
- **Customizable thresholds**: The ratio tolerances can be loosened or tightened, which changes how many patterns qualify.
- **Multi-timeframe support**: Designed to work across timeframes, with the caveat that lower timeframes tend to produce more noise than higher ones.

**Settings and How to Tune Them**

- **Timeframe**: Higher timeframes suit swing trading; lower timeframes produce more noise and weaker patterns.
- **Retracement tolerance**: The default tolerance is 3%. Widening it admits more patterns; tightening it admits fewer.
- **Show PRZ only**: A toggle that hides the pattern lines and leaves only the reversal zone, for a cleaner chart.
- **Alert on completion**: An alert that fires when the C-D leg is fully formed.

**How to Use It for Entries and Exits**

A typical workflow:

1. **Wait for the pattern to complete** — The indicator draws the full structure when all Fibonacci conditions are met. Entering before completion means trading an unconfirmed structure.
2. **Entry**: Place a limit order at the D point (the PRZ). The Cypher's D is typically a 0.786 retracement of X-C. If price touches that level and prints a reversal candle (pin bar, engulfing), that is the trigger.
3. **Stop loss**: Place it just beyond the D point. If the pattern fails, the loss should be tight.
4. **Take profit**: Target the A point (first leg) or the B point (second leg). Splitting the position between the two is one common approach.

**Honest Pros and Cons**

**Pros**:
- Automates a manual process that many traders struggle with (harmonic ratios).
- The PRZ shading is genuinely useful—no squinting at tiny numbers.
- Works best on higher timeframes.
- Free (no paid version, no hidden costs).

**Cons**:
- Does *not* filter by trend context. A Cypher in a strong trend can fail hard. Market structure must be checked separately.
- No built-in volume or momentum confirmation. An additional momentum indicator is a reasonable supplement.
- Can repaint. The pattern may disappear if price moves beyond the tolerance after initial detection. This is a common issue with harmonic indicators—always wait for a confirmed candle close at D.

**Who It's Actually For**

This is for swing traders who already understand harmonic patterns but don't want to draw them manually. Scalpers and day traders should skip it—the pattern takes too long to form. Beginners still learning Fibonacci can use it to *see* the structure, but should not trade it blindly.

**FAQ Addressing Real Trader Questions**

*Q: Does it work on crypto?*  
A: Yes, but higher timeframes are more reliable for Bitcoin and Ethereum. Altcoins are volatile enough that false patterns appear frequently.

*Q: Can I use it with ICT concepts?*  
A: The Cypher's PRZ aligns well with order blocks. Overlaying a supply/demand indicator is one way to confirm.

*Q: Why does the pattern disappear after a few candles?*  
A: Repainting. Price exceeded the ratio tolerance, so the indicator invalidated the pattern. That behavior also prevents holding a setup that has already failed.

**Final Verdict with Star Rating**

Cypher_Pattern delivers what it promises: automatic Cypher pattern detection with clear PRZs. It is not a magic wand—trend analysis and risk management still sit with the trader—but it removes the manual work. For harmonic traders who want a dedicated, free tool, this is a solid addition to the toolbox.
