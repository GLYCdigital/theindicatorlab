If you've ever stared at a chart trying to manually draw Fibonacci extensions for a WXY correction pattern, you know the pain. This indicator handles that grunt work. It automatically identifies W-X-Y structures (common in Elliott Wave theory) and plots potential target zones based on the pattern's geometry. No guessing, no ruler tool. Just clean, objective levels.

The developer recommends running it on MACD charts, where it's intended to help catch continuation moves after complex corrections. Here's what you need to know before installing it.

**What it actually does**

The indicator scans price action for three-swing patterns where waves W and Y move in the same direction, connected by an X wave (counter-trend). Once identified, it draws horizontal target lines using the length of wave W projected from the end of wave Y. You get two key levels: a primary target and an extended target, with the extended ratio selectable in the settings. It's a pure trend-following tool — not a reversal predictor.

**Key features that stand out**

First, the auto-detection is clean. Unlike some pattern tools that flood your chart with noise, this one only draws targets when a valid WXY structure completes. Second, you can toggle between Fibonacci ratios in the settings for the extended target. Third, the labels are customizable: change their color, size, and position so they don't overlap with your price action.

**Settings and How to Tune Them**

- **Timeframe:** The developer recommends MACD charts. Higher timeframes tend to produce cleaner structures; very low timeframes are more prone to noise.
- **Wave sensitivity:** Adjusts how strictly the indicator qualifies a WXY structure. Stricter settings filter more patterns out; looser settings let more through.
- **Target ratio:** Selects the Fibonacci ratio used for the extended target, alongside the primary target based on wave W's length.
- **Show labels:** Toggles the on-chart labels. Their color, size, and position are adjustable so they don't overlap price action.

**How to actually use it**

Entry logic is straightforward: wait for the indicator to plot a target after the Y wave completes. Don't enter immediately — let price confirm with a breakout of the X wave's high/low. The cleanest entries tend to come after a decisive break of that X wave level, ideally with volume supporting the move.

Set your stop loss just beyond the end of wave Y. Take partial profits at the primary target, then trail the rest toward the extended target. Adding a longer moving average, such as a 200 EMA, can serve as extra trend confirmation.

**Pros & Cons**

*Pros:*
- Saves hours of manual pattern drawing
- Suited to strong trends
- Clear, objective levels — no interpretation needed

*Cons:*
- Not useful in ranging markets, where it will draw targets that never hit
- Doesn't account for volume or momentum — you need to filter trades yourself
- The "auto" part can lag; sometimes the pattern completes, but price reverses before the target is plotted

**Who it's for**

This is for traders who already understand WXY patterns and want to automate the target calculation. If you trade Elliott Wave or harmonic patterns and use trend-following strategies, it's a time-saver. Beginners will struggle because the indicator doesn't explain *why* a pattern is valid — it just draws lines.

**Alternatives worth considering**

- **Auto Fibonacci Retracements** (free) — gives you standard fib levels without pattern detection. Better for simple trends.
- **Patternz** (paid) — detects more pattern types (ABCD, Gartley, etc.) but is noisier.
- **Manual drawing** — if you only trade one or two patterns, you don't need this tool.

**FAQ**

**Does it work on crypto?** Yes, but it behaves best on higher timeframes. Crypto's volatility triggers false patterns on lower ones.

**Can I use it with MACD?** The developer shows it on MACD charts, where it helps confirm momentum. It also applies to regular candlestick charts.

**Why don't targets always get hit?** The indicator projects targets based on wave geometry, not market psychology. Always use a stop loss.

**Final Verdict**

W_X_Y_Pattern_Auto_Targets_Jpt does one thing well — automate WXY target drawing — and saves time for experienced traders. It's not a magic bullet, and it won't help you in choppy markets. But if you trade trends and understand the pattern, it's a reliable assistant. Just don't expect it to predict the future. No indicator does.
