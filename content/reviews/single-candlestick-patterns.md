# Single_Candlestick_Patterns Review

Most candlestick pattern indicators on TradingView are noisy — they paint dozens of arrows per bar and call it analysis. Single_Candlestick_Patterns takes a different approach. It does one thing: identifies single-candle reversal and continuation signals, and presents them cleanly.

The premise is simple. Instead of flooding your chart with every doji, hammer, and spinning top ever printed, this indicator is designed around single-candle patterns evaluated in the context of trend. It marks bullish and bearish setups with distinct arrows and color-coded candle backgrounds. The stated design intent is clean pattern recognition on the closed candle, without lagging line overlays.

**What sets it apart**

Most pattern scanners treat a hammer at the top of an uptrend the same as a hammer in a downtrend. This one is built to evaluate each signal against the prevailing trend direction, which can be toggled in settings. A bullish pattern in a downtrend is flagged differently than one appearing after a pullback in an uptrend. That contextual filtering is the core differentiator versus generic scanners.

The signal quality scoring is another distinction. Each pattern receives a strength rating based on body-to-wick ratio, position within the recent range, and volume confirmation. A minimum score threshold can be set to filter out weaker patterns — a feature many free alternatives lack entirely.

**Settings and How to Tune Them**

The defaults are a starting point, not a finished configuration. Key settings to consider:

- **Min Signal Strength** — Controls how many patterns pass through. A higher threshold produces fewer but more selective signals; a lower one catches more marginal setups.
- **Trend Filter** — Toggle for contextual filtering against prevailing trend. Trading against the filter defeats the indicator's stated purpose.
- **Patterns** — Individual pattern types can be enabled or disabled. Spinning Top and Doji can be turned off if you want to reduce noise; Marubozu, Hammer/Hanging Man, Shooting Star, and Engulfing can be kept on.
- **Timeframe** — The indicator is designed for higher timeframes where each candle represents meaningful price action. Lower timeframes tend to produce clustered signals.

**How it's used**

The logic is straightforward. In an uptrend, look for bullish continuation or reversal signals on a pullback candle. Entry at the close of the signal candle, stop loss below the pattern's low, target the previous swing high. For bearish setups, reverse it.

Patience matters. This indicator produces a limited number of quality signals per week on a single pair. Forcing trades on every arrow it prints is a common failure mode. Higher signal strength readings correspond to more selective setups.

**The honest trade-offs**

Pros:
- Clean, uncluttered chart output — no rainbow clouds or meaningless histograms
- Context-aware signals are designed to reduce false positives
- Signal strength scoring helps rank setups objectively
- Stated as non-repainting — alerts fire on confirmed candle closes only

Cons:
- Single-candle patterns alone aren't sufficient for entries. Independent trend confirmation is still required.
- The extensive settings menu is overwhelming at first glance and takes time to dial in.
- The pattern list is narrower than some competitors despite the name suggesting broader coverage.
- Performance on lower timeframes is weaker — this is positioned as a swing trading tool, not a scalping one.

**Who should install it**

This indicator suits swing traders and position traders who already have a trend framework and want an objective candlestick confirmation layer. If you trade higher timeframes, it can earn its place in your toolkit. Day traders working very short timeframes should look elsewhere — the signal-to-noise ratio isn't there.

**Alternatives worth considering**

If you need multi-candle patterns, "All Candlestick Patterns" by LuxAlgo is more comprehensive, though noisier. For pure price action with trend context, this could be paired with a simple EMA ribbon — but the signal scoring here adds a quantifiable layer that manual analysis lacks.

**FAQ**

**Does it repaint?** The indicator is stated to be non-repainting. Signals appear only after the candle closes and remain fixed.

**Can I get alerts?** Yes, it supports native TradingView alerts for both bullish and bearish signals.

**Does it work on crypto?** It works on any market with adequate volume.

**What's the best timeframe?** Higher timeframes give the most reliable signals. Lower timeframes are not where this tool is designed to perform.

**Final verdict**

Single_Candlestick_Patterns won't replace your trading strategy, and it doesn't pretend to. What it does is give you a clean, context-aware read on single-candle patterns with a useful strength score. The trend filter and quality thresholds separate it from the pattern-scanner crowd, and the stated lack of repainting makes it suitable for live trading.

It's not perfect. The settings menu needs simplification, and the pattern list is narrower than competitors. But for what it's designed to do — confirm trend entries with single-candle signals — it's a solid tool. If you're a swing trader who wants to cut through candle pattern noise, this is worth the install. Just don't expect it to make trading decisions for you.

**Rating: 4/5 — Recommended for disciplined swing traders.**
